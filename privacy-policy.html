Section 02

🔧 How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively to improve your experience on BullBear Market. Specifically:

  • Account creation and management — to create and maintain your profile
  • Platform improvement — to analyse how users interact with the platform and improve features
  • Customer support — to respond to your queries and resolve issues efficiently
  • Contest & leaderboard participation — to track your virtual trading performance
  • Communication — important platform updates, policy changes, contest results
🔒 We use your data ONLY to enhance your experience. Nothing else.
Section 03

🚫 No Data Selling — Ever

BullBear Market will never sell, rent, lease or otherwise transfer your personal data to any third party for commercial or profit purposes.

✅ Your data belongs to you. It stays private within our platform — period.

We are not in the business of selling user data. Our platform is monetised through advertising shown to users, not through selling user information to advertisers or data brokers.

Section 04

🔐 Data Security

We implement strong, industry-standard security practices to protect your personal data from unauthorised access, loss or misuse:

  • Secure servers — data stored on protected, access-controlled servers
  • Encrypted communication — all data transmitted via HTTPS/SSL encryption
  • Regular security monitoring — systems monitored continuously for threats
  • Limited access — only authorised personnel can access user data
  • No third-party data sales — no backdoors or data sharing for profit
⚠️ While we implement strong security, no system is 100% breach-proof. We recommend using a strong, unique password for your account.
Section 05

🗑️ Account Deletion

You have full control over your data. You can request complete account deletion at any time by contacting our support team.

  • All personal data permanently deleted — name, email, phone, profile info
  • No data retained — nothing is kept after deletion is processed
  • Virtual coins & leaderboard history — also permanently deleted
  • Irreversible — this action cannot be undone once processed
📩 To delete your account, email us at moneymaker.earnmoney@gmail.com with subject "Account Deletion Request" and your registered email.
Section 06

💰 No Real Money Involvement

BullBear Market is a purely educational and simulation platform. This means:

  • No real money trading — all trades are simulated with virtual coins
  • No financial transactions — no payments, deposits or withdrawals inside the simulation
  • No real monetary value — virtual coins cannot be withdrawn or exchanged for real money
  • No payment data collected — we do not store any credit card, debit card, UPI or banking information
🛡️ BullBear Market is 100% safe — no real money, no financial risk at any point.

🔗 Third Party Services

To operate and improve BullBear Market, we may use certain third-party services. These may include:
