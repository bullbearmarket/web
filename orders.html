Order Types in Trading – Complete Beginner to Advanced Guide

Agar tum trading start kar rahe ho ya already kar rahe ho, to ek cheez sabse important hoti hai — Order Types. Yeh decide karta hai ki tum market me entry kaise loge aur exit kaise karoge.

📌 What is Order in Trading?

Simple language me samjho — order ka matlab hota hai broker ko instruction dena ki tum kis price par buy ya sell karna chahte ho.

Example: Agar tum bolte ho “Mujhe Reliance ka share abhi ke abhi lena hai” → yeh ek order hai.

🔥 Types of Orders

1. Market Order

Market order sabse simple hota hai. Isme tum current market price par instantly buy ya sell karte ho.

Example:
Stock price = ₹100
Tumne market order diya → trade instantly ₹100 ke around execute ho jayega.

Use kab kare?
✔ Jab tumhe immediate entry chahiye
✔ Fast market me

Risk:
❌ Slippage ho sakta hai (price thoda upar/neeche mil sakta hai)

2. Limit Order

Limit order me tum apna price set karte ho.

Example:
Stock price = ₹100
Tum bolte ho → “Mujhe ₹95 par hi buy karna hai”

Agar price ₹95 tak aata hai tabhi order execute hoga.

Best for:
✔ Controlled entry
✔ Swing trading

3. Stop Loss Order

Stop loss tumhari safety hai.

Example:
Buy price = ₹100
Stop loss = ₹90

Agar price ₹90 tak girta hai → automatic sell ho jayega.

Important:
Trading me survive karna hai to stop loss mandatory hai.

4. Stop Limit Order

Yeh advanced version hai stop loss ka.

Isme tum 2 price set karte ho: - Trigger price - Limit price

Example: Trigger = ₹95
Limit = ₹94

💡 Real Life Strategy Example

Agar tum beginner ho to yeh simple rule follow karo:

  • Entry → Limit Order
  • Exit → Stop Loss
  • Fast trade → Market Order

Yeh combination tumhe safe rakhega.

⚠️ Common Mistakes

  • Stop loss use na karna
  • Market order blindly use karna
  • Emotions me trade lena

📈 Pro Tips

  • Always risk manage karo
  • Small capital se start karo
  • Learning pe focus karo, earning pe nahi

🧠 Conclusion

Order types trading ka foundation hai. Agar tumhe yeh samajh aa gaya, to tum trading me 90% beginners se aage ho.

Simple rakho, disciplined raho aur har trade me stop loss lagao.
