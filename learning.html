Stock Market Quick Cheatsheet

Instant reference for patterns, technical formulas, Greeks, and risk rules.

Hammer Candle Bullish Reversal

Long lower wick (2x body) at the bottom of a downtrend. Indicates aggressive rejection of lower prices by buyers.

Morning Star Strong 3-Candle

Bearish candle + Small indecision candle + Strong green candle closing above midpoint. Signals major trend bottom.

Bullish Engulfing Momentum Shift

A large green body completely envelops the prior small red body with surging volume. High probability buy setup.

Piercing Line Deep Reversal

Opens below prior red low but surges to close more than 50% into the red candle body. Signals dip-buying.

Shooting Star Bearish Top

Long upper shadow at resistance after an uptrend. Shows buyers tried to push up but sellers aggressively took control.

Evening Star 3-Bar Reversal

Green candle + Small star + Heavy red candle confirming sellers. High probability short setup on indices.

Bearish Engulfing Seller Takeover

Large red candle completely swallows the preceding green candle at resistance. Signals momentum flip.

Dark Cloud Cover Resistance Trap

Opens above prior green candle's high but dumps to close below its midpoint. Classic bull trap confirmation.

RSI Divergence Momentum Lead

Price makes Higher High but RSI makes Lower High (Bearish Divergence). Signals weakening trend before reversal.

MACD Golden Cross Trend Shift

MACD line crosses above the Signal line from below the zero line. Signals initiation of medium-term upside rally.

20/50/200 EMA Trend Hierarchy

20 EMA for short-term pullbacks, 50 EMA for swing support, 200 EMA for institutional bull/bear boundary.

Bollinger Band Squeeze Volatility Expansion

Bands contract to lowest width in 20 periods, preceding an explosive multi-hundred point breakout on NIFTY.

Delta (Δ) Price Sensitivity

Measures how much the option price moves per ₹1 change in underlying index. ATM options typically have a 0.50 Delta.

Theta (θ) Time Decay Enemy

The daily rate of premium erosion. Accelerates exponentially in the final 48 hours before weekly Thursday expiry.

Gamma (Γ) Delta Acceleration

Rate of change of Delta. Causes ATM options on expiry day to explode from ₹10 to ₹100 during fast scalping rallies.

Vega (ν) Volatility Shock

Sensitivity to India VIX changes. When VIX spikes (e.g. Budget or Election), option premiums rise even without price movement.

The 1% Capital Rule Survival Law

Never risk more than 1% of account balance on a single trade. On ₹1,00,000 capital, max loss is ₹1,000 per trade.

1:2 Risk-Reward Ratio Math of Profit

With a 1:2 setup, you only need a 35% win rate to remain solidly profitable over a 100-trade sample size.

Daily Max 3 Trades Overtrading Trap

Cap intraday trades to 3 maximum. Overtrading creates emotional fatigue, leading to impulsive revenge entries.

No Stop Loss, No Trade Zero Compromise

Never enter an order without pre-defining the exact stop loss level. Hope is not a trading strategy.
