2. What is Paper Trading? Complete Mechanics

Historically, in the 1980s and 1990s on Dalal Street and Wall Street, traders literally used pen and a sheet of paper to write down stock prices, jot their entry price, target, and stop-loss, and manually track whether they made a profit or loss when prices fluctuated. This was the origin of the phrase "Paper Trading".

In 2026, modern technology has revolutionized this process. 6. Mathematical Risk Management: The Rules That Keep You Alive

Professional trading is fundamentally a game of risk management, not prediction. Here are the core mathematical rules you must drill into your subconscious using paper trading:

📐 The 1% Golden Rule of Capital Protection

Never risk more than 1% of your total trading capital on any single trade setup. If your virtual account balance is ₹1,00,000, your maximum stop-loss risk per trade is exactly ₹1,000.

Position Size Formula:

Quantity = Maximum Acceptable Risk (₹) / (Entry Price - Stop Loss Price)

⚖️ The Minimum 1:2 Risk-to-Reward Ratio (RRR)

Ensure that for every ₹1 of risk you take, your potential target delivers at least ₹2 of profit. Even with a humble 40% win rate, a 1:2 RRR ensures long-term profitability:

  • 10 Trades | 4 Wins (4 × ₹2,000 = +₹8,000)
  • 6 Losses (6 × ₹1,000 = -₹6,000)
  • Net Profit = +₹2,000 despite losing more trades than you won!
