📊 Complete Stock Market Guide for Beginners

Learn Stock Market from Zero to Advanced Level — Simple Hindi + English Guide

🚀 What is Stock Market?

Stock Market ek financial marketplace hai jahan par companies apne shares sell karti hain aur investors un shares ko buy aur sell karte hain. Simple language me samjhe to: Stock Market = Business ka ownership buy karna

Jab aap kisi company ka share kharidte ho, iska matlab hai aap us company ke ek chhote owner ban jaate ho. Agar company grow karti hai → aapka paisa grow hota hai Agar company girti hai → aapka paisa bhi girta hai

⚙️ How Stock Market Works?

Stock market demand aur supply ke principle par kaam karta hai.

  • Buyer jyada → Price up 📈
  • Seller jyada → Price down 📉

India me major exchanges:

  • NSE (National Stock Exchange)
  • BSE (Bombay Stock Exchange)

Trading brokers ke through hoti hai jaise: Zerodha, Upstox, Angel One etc.

📌 Types of Trading

1. Intraday Trading

Same day buy & sell. High risk but fast profit.

2. Swing Trading

Few days to weeks holding.

3. Long Term Investing

Years tak hold karke wealth build karna.

4. Scalping

Small small profits in minutes.

📊 Technical Analysis

Technical analysis ka matlab hai charts aur indicators ke through price movement ko samajhna.

  • Candlestick Patterns
  • Support & Resistance
  • Trend Lines
  • Indicators (RSI, MACD)

🎯 Trading Strategy

Ek successful trader banne ke liye strategy hona mandatory hai.

  • Entry Rule
  • Exit Rule
  • Stop Loss
  • Risk Management

Without strategy trading = gambling ❌

⚠️ Risk Management

Stock market me survival ka sabse bada rule: Capital bachao

  • Never risk more than 2% per trade
  • Always use Stop Loss
  • Overtrading avoid karo

❌ Common Mistakes

  • Emotional trading
  • Overconfidence
  • No strategy
  • News ke peeche blindly bhagna

🏁 Conclusion

Stock market ek powerful tool hai wealth create karne ka, lekin bina knowledge ke ye dangerous bhi ho sakta hai.
