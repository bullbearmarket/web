📊 What is Volume?

Volume का मतलब है एक निश्चित समय में कितने shares trade हुए।

  • High volume → Strong interest
  • Low volume → Weak movement
---

🔥 Why Volume is Important

  • Trend confirmation
  • Breakout validation
  • Fake move पहचानना
---

📈 Price + Volume Relationship

1. Price Up + Volume Up

Strong bullish signal

2. Price Up + Volume Down

Weak trend (reversal possible)

3. Price Down + Volume Up

Strong bearish

4. Price Down + Volume Low

Weak selling

---

📊 Volume Breakout Strategy

जब price resistance तोड़ता है और volume बढ़ता है → Strong breakout

---

📉 Fake Breakout कैसे पहचानें

  • Price breakout लेकिन volume low
  • Immediate reversal

🧠 Psychology Behind Volume

Volume crowd behavior दिखाता है।

  • High volume → Fear or greed
  • Low volume → Confusion
---

📊 Volume + Price Strategy

  • Breakout + volume spike → Entry
  • Low volume → Avoid
---
