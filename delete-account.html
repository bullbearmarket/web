Delete Your Account

Option 1: Delete directly from App (Recommended)

1. Open BullBear Market App
2. Go to Account Settings
3. Click on "Delete My Account"
4. Confirm deletion

✔ Your account will be deleted instantly from our system.

Option 2: Request via WhatsApp or Email

If you are unable to access the app, you can request account deletion:

Chat on WhatsApp

Send Email

Please include your registered username/email in your request.

What happens when you delete your account?

  • Your account will be permanently deleted
  • Your personal data (name, email) will be removed
  • Your financial/withdrawal data will be deleted
  • Some data may be retained for legal/security purposes (if required)

Important Notes

  • Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone
  • Processing time: Instant (via app) or up to 48 hours (manual request)
