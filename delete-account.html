Delete Your Account

You can permanently delete your account anytime. Follow the steps below.

Delete from App (Recommended)

1. Open BullBear Market App
2. Go to Account Settings
3. Tap "Delete My Account"
4. Confirm deletion

✔ Instant deletion when done through the app.

Request via WhatsApp or Email

If you cannot access the app, you can request deletion manually:

Chat on WhatsApp

Send Email

Include your registered email or username.

What happens when you delete your account?

  • Your account is permanently deleted
  • Personal data (name, email) is removed
  • Financial/withdrawal data is deleted
  • Some data may be retained for legal/security purposes (up to 90 days)

Important Notes

  • This action cannot be undone
  • App deletion = instant
  • Manual request = up to 48 hours
