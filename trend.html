📊 What is Trend?

Trend का मतलब है market की direction।

  • Uptrend → Higher highs & higher lows
  • Downtrend → Lower highs & lower lows
  • Sideways → Range market
---

🔥 Types of Trends

1. Uptrend

Market ऊपर जा रहा है

2. Downtrend

Market नीचे जा रहा है

3. Sideways Trend

No clear direction

---

📈 Trend Structure

  • Higher High (HH)
  • Higher Low (HL)
  • Lower High (LH)
  • Lower Low (LL)
Structure समझना = Trend master करना
---

📊 How to Identify Trend

1. Price Action

Candles और structure देखो

2. Trendlines

Support और resistance draw करो

3. Moving Average

Trend direction confirm करो

📊 Trendline Strategy

Trendline connect करती है important highs या lows को।

  • Uptrend → trendline support
  • Downtrend → trendline resistance
---

📊 Moving Average Strategy
