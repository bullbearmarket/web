Trading Strategy – Complete System Guide (Hindi + English)

Trading me success sirf indicator ya ek strategy se nahi aata, balki ek complete trading system se aata hai.

Simple language me: 👉 Strategy = plan 👉 System = full execution process

Agar tumhare paas system nahi hai, to tum gambling kar rahe ho — trading nahi.

📌 What is Trading Strategy?

Trading strategy ek predefined plan hota hai jo batata hai:

  • Entry kab leni hai
  • Exit kab karna hai
  • Stop loss kaha lagana hai
  • Profit target kya hoga

Yeh rules tumhe emotional trading se bachate hain.

🔥 What is Complete Trading System?

Complete system me 5 major parts hote hain:

  • Market Selection
  • Entry Strategy
  • Exit Strategy
  • Risk Management
  • Trading Psychology

Inme se koi bhi missing hai → system incomplete hai.

📊 1. Market Selection

Sabse pehle decide karo:

  • Stock, Index ya Crypto?
  • Intraday ya Swing?

👉 High liquidity market choose karo (Nifty, Bank Nifty)

📈 2. Entry Strategy

Entry clear honi chahiye — guess nahi.

Example:

  • Support se bounce → Buy
  • Resistance break → Buy
  • Trendline break → Entry

👉 Indicators use kar sakte ho: - Moving Average - RSI - MACD

🚪 3. Exit Strategy

Most traders yahi galti karte hain — exit plan nahi hota.

  • Stop Loss fix hona chahiye
  • Target defined hona chahiye
  • Trailing stop use karo

⚠️ 4. Risk Management

Rule: 👉 Ek trade me capital ka 1–2% risk karo

Example: ₹1,00,000 capital → ₹1,000–₹2,000 risk

👉 Risk management hi survival hai

🧠 5. Trading Psychology

Mindset sabse important factor hai:

  • Greed control karo
  • Loss accept karo
  • Discipline maintain karo
  • Overtrading avoid karo

🔥 Types of Trading Strategies

1. Scalping Strategy

Small profits, fast trades

2. Intraday Strategy

Same day buy-sell

3. Swing Trading

2–10 days hold

4. Breakout Strategy

Resistance break hone par entry

5. Trend Following

Trend ke saath trade

📌 Example of Complete Trading System

System Example:

  • Market: Nifty
  • Timeframe: 5 min
  • Entry: Resistance breakout
  • Stop Loss: Previous candle low
  • Target: 1:2 Risk Reward

👉 Yeh ek proper system hai (clear rules)

🚫 Common Mistakes

  • Strategy change karte rehna
  • No stop loss
  • Overtrading
  • Copy trading without understanding

💎 Pro Tips (Advanced Level)

  • Backtesting karo
  • Trading journal maintain karo
  • Consistency pe focus karo
  • 1 strategy master karo

📊 Strategy vs System

Strategy = Entry rule
System = Full plan (entry + exit + risk + psychology)

👉 Successful trader system follow karta hai

🧾 Conclusion

Trading me success luck se nahi, system se aata hai.

👉 Clear rules banao
👉 Discipline follow karo
👉 Emotions control karo

“Plan your trade, trade your plan.”
