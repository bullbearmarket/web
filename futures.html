Futures Trading – Complete Guide (Hindi + English)

Agar tum trading me serious ho ya stock market ko deeply samajhna chahte ho, to Futures Trading ek bahut powerful concept hai.

Simple language me: Futures ka matlab hota hai future me kisi asset ko buy ya sell karne ka contract.

Yeh beginners ke liye thoda complex lagta hai, lekin agar ek baar samajh aa gaya, to tum professional level trading kar sakte ho.

📌 What is Futures Trading?

Futures ek contract hota hai jisme tum future date par ek asset (stock, index, commodity) ko predefined price par buy ya sell karte ho.

Example:
Aaj Nifty = 20,000 hai
Tumne futures contract buy kiya → expectation: price badhega

Agar price future me 20,500 ho gaya → profit
Agar 19,500 ho gaya → loss

🔥 How Futures Work (Kaise kaam karta hai)

Futures trading leverage par kaam karti hai.

Matlab: Tumhe full amount nahi dena hota — sirf margin dena hota hai.

Example:
1 lot = ₹5,00,000 value
Tumhe sirf ₹1,00,000 margin dena padta hai

Yahan se profit bhi bada hota hai aur loss bhi.

💡 Key Features of Futures Trading

  • Leverage (kam paiso me bada trade)
  • Hedging (risk reduce karna)
  • High liquidity
  • Expiry date hoti hai
  • Daily mark-to-market settlement

📊 Types of Futures

  • Stock Futures (Reliance, TCS)
  • Index Futures (Nifty, Bank Nifty)
  • Commodity Futures (Gold, Silver)
  • Currency Futures (USD/INR)

⚠️ Risk in Futures Trading

Yeh sabse important section hai — Futures me risk high hota hai.

1. Leverage Risk

Small move → big loss ya profit

2. Margin Call

Agar loss badh gaya → broker extra paisa maangega

3. Volatility

Price bahut fast move karta hai

4. Emotional Risk

Fear aur greed sabse bada enemy hai

📈 Real Example (Practical समझो)

Nifty futures buy kiya at 20,000
Lot size = 50

Price 100 point upar gaya → Profit = ₹5,000
Price 100 point neeche gaya → Loss = ₹5,000

Isi liye futures powerful bhi hai aur dangerous bhi.

🧠 Strategy for Beginners

  • Always stop loss use karo
  • Over leverage mat lo
  • Trend follow karo
  • Small capital se start karo

🚫 Common Mistakes

  • Without knowledge futures trading start karna
  • Stop loss ignore karna
  • Revenge trading
  • Over trading

💎 Pro Tips (Advanced Level)

  • Hedging use karo
  • Options + futures combine karo
  • Risk reward ratio maintain karo
  • Market structure samjho

📌 Futures vs Normal Trading

Normal trading me tum full amount dete ho.
Futures me leverage use hota hai.

Normal trading safe hai
Futures high risk high reward hai

🧾 Conclusion

Futures trading ek powerful tool hai, lekin sirf tab jab tum discipline aur knowledge ke saath use karo.

Agar tum beginner ho: 👉 Pehle learn karo
👉 Phir practice karo
👉 Phir small capital se start karo

Remember: Trading me survive karna sabse important hai.
