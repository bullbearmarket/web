Gap Trading Strategy – Complete Guide (Hindi + English)

Gap Trading ek powerful intraday strategy hai jo market ke opening par hone wale price gaps par based hoti hai.

Simple language me: 👉 Jab market previous close se upar ya neeche open hota hai → use gap kehte hain

Aur isi gap ko use karke traders profit banate hain.

📌 What is Gap in Trading?

Gap ka matlab hota hai price ka ek level se direct doosre level par jump karna, beech me koi trading nahi hoti.

Example:
Yesterday Close = ₹100
Today Open = ₹105 → Gap Up
Today Open = ₹95 → Gap Down

📊 Types of Gaps

1. Gap Up

Market higher open hota hai → bullish signal

2. Gap Down

Market lower open hota hai → bearish signal

3. Common Gap

Small gap jo jaldi fill ho jata hai

4. Breakaway Gap

Strong trend start hone ka signal

5. Exhaustion Gap

Trend ke end ka signal

📈 Gap Trading ka Basic Concept

Gap trading me sabse important concept hai: 👉 Gap fill hoga ya nahi?

Market 2 tarah behave karta hai:

  • Gap Fill → price wapas previous close tak aata hai
  • Gap Continuation → price same direction me move karta hai

🔥 Gap Trading Strategy (Beginner Friendly)

Strategy 1: Gap Fill Strategy

👉 Jab gap small ho aur volume low ho:

  • Gap up → Sell
  • Gap down → Buy

Target: Previous close price

Strategy 2: Gap Continuation

👉 Jab gap strong ho + high volume:

  • Gap up → Buy
  • Gap down → Sell

Trend follow karo

📊 Indicators for Gap Trading

Moving Average

Trend confirmation ke liye

RSI

Overbought / Oversold check karne ke liye

Support & Resistance

Entry aur exit ke liye best levels

⚠️ Risk in Gap Trading

1. Fake Breakout

Market opposite direction me move kar sakta hai

2. High Volatility

Opening time par movement fast hota hai

3. Slippage

Order expected price par execute nahi hota

4. Emotional Trading

Fast decisions → galti hone ke chances zyada

🧠 Psychology (Most Important)

Gap trading me discipline sabse important hai:

  • Wait for confirmation
  • FOMO avoid karo
  • Stop loss follow karo
  • Overtrading mat karo

🚫 Common Mistakes

  • Market open hote hi trade lena
  • Without confirmation entry
  • News ignore karna
  • Stop loss na lagana

💎 Pro Tips (Advanced Level)

  • First 5–15 min observe karo
  • High volume stocks select karo
  • Index direction check karo (Nifty/Bank Nifty)
  • Risk reward maintain karo (1:2 minimum)

📌 Real Example

Stock previous close = ₹100
Today open = ₹105 (Gap Up)

Agar weak volume hai → Sell karo → target ₹100
Agar strong volume hai → Buy karo → trend follow karo

🧾 Conclusion

Gap trading ek powerful intraday strategy hai, lekin sirf tab jab tum discipline aur patience ke saath trade karo.

👉 Har gap tradeable nahi hota
👉 Confirmation sabse important hai

Smart trader wahi hai jo wait karta hai.
